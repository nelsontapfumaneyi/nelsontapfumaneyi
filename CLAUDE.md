# Publication safety
- Do not include Claude-Session trailers, interactive AI session URLs, browser cookies, authentication headers, signed download links, credentials or personal local paths in files or commit messages.
- Share only static, redacted research outputs. Retain public source citations and appropriate authorship attribution.
- Before committing, inspect staged changes and the proposed commit message; run the disclosure guard described in SECURITY.md.
- Do not rewrite shared history, rotate credentials, change visibility or delete published research without specific authorization.
